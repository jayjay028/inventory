1. Getting Started

Logging In

  1. Open your browser and navigate to the system URL
  2. Enter your Username and Password
  3. Click Sign In
Your administrator will provide your login credentials. Change your password after first login.

Navigation

The system has a sidebar menu on the left with the following sections:

  • Dashboard — Overview of your business
  • POS Terminal — Point of Sale cashier screen
  • Inventory — Items, Categories, Stock operations
  • Sales — Sales history and shift management
  • Contacts — Customers and Suppliers
  • Reports — PDF report generation
  • Administration — Users, Settings, Audit Trail
Menu items are shown based on your permissions. You may not see all sections depending on your role.

UsernamePasswordRole
adminadminAdministrator (full access)

2. Dashboard

The dashboard provides a quick overview of your business:

  • Total Items — Number of active items in your inventory
  • Stock Value — Total value of inventory (cost × quantity)
  • Low Stock Alerts — Items at or below reorder level
  • Today's Sales — Sales count and revenue for today
  • Pending Approvals — Stock transactions waiting for approval

Dashboard Sections

  • Recent Transactions — Last 10 stock transactions
  • Low Stock Alerts — Items that need restocking
  • Top Selling Items — Best sellers this month

3. Categories

Categories help organize your items (e.g., Electronics, Food & Beverages, Office Supplies).

Create a Category

  1. Go to Inventory → Categories
  2. Click New Category
  3. Enter the category Name (required) and optional Description
  4. Click Create

Edit / Deactivate

  • Click Edit on a category row to modify it
  • Click Deactivate to hide it from dropdowns (items remain assigned)
Deactivated categories are not deleted — they can be reactivated anytime.

5. Customers

Manage customers for sales transactions, Official Receipts (OR), and Sales Invoices (SI).

Create a Customer

  1. Go to Contacts → Customers
  2. Click New Customer
  3. Fill in: Name (required), TIN, Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email
  4. Click Create
Customers are needed when issuing Official Receipts (OR) or Sales Invoices (SI) per BIR requirements.

6. Suppliers

Manage suppliers for purchase tracking and stock-in transactions.

Create a Supplier

  1. Go to Contacts → Suppliers
  2. Click New Supplier
  3. Fill in: Name (required), TIN, Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email
  4. Click Create

7. Stock Operations

Stock Overview

Go to Inventory → Stock Overview to see current stock levels for all items.

Stock status indicators:

  • NORMAL — Quantity above reorder level
  • LOW — At or below reorder level
  • OUT OF STOCK — Zero quantity

7.1 Stock In (Receiving Goods)

Create Transaction → Status: CREATED → Approve → Stock Updated ✓
  1. Go to Inventory → Stock In
  2. Select the Item (current stock shown)
  3. Enter Quantity and Unit Cost
  4. Optionally select a Supplier
  5. Choose Document Type (PO, RR, or None)
  6. Toggle Apply Tax if applicable
  7. Add Remarks and Add-ons (shipping, handling) if needed
  8. Click Submit
Stock is NOT updated immediately. The transaction must be Approved first.

7.2 Stock Out (Releasing Goods)

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Out
  2. Select the Item (validates sufficient stock)
  3. Enter Quantity and Unit Price
  4. Optionally select a Customer
  5. Apply Discount if applicable (Fixed or Percentage)
  6. Choose Document Type (OR, SI, DR, or None)
  7. Click Submit

7.3 Stock Adjustment

Use for physical count corrections or inventory adjustments.

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Adjustment
  2. Select the Item
  3. Enter the correct Quantity
  4. Add a Reason/Remarks (required for audit)
  5. Click Submit

7.4 Transaction Approval

All stock transactions go through an approval workflow:

StatusMeaningStock Impact
CREATEDPending approvalNone
APPROVEDApproved and processedStock updated
CANCELLEDCancelled/rejectedNone

To approve or cancel:

  1. Go to Inventory → Transactions
  2. Filter by Status: CREATED
  3. Click Approve or Cancel on the transaction
Requires APPROVE_TRANSACTIONS or CANCEL_TRANSACTIONS permission.

8. POS Terminal

The Point of Sale terminal is a full-screen cashier interface for processing sales.

8.1 Shift Management

Before making sales, you must open a shift:

Opening a Shift

  1. Navigate to POS Terminal
  2. If no shift is open, you'll be prompted to open one
  3. Enter the Opening Amount (cash in drawer)
  4. Click Open Shift

Closing a Shift

  1. Go to Sales → Shifts or use the shift close option in POS
  2. Count your cash and enter the Closing Amount
  3. The system calculates Expected Amount and shows any Over/Short
  4. Add optional Remarks
  5. Click Close Shift
Only ONE shift can be open per cashier at a time.

8.2 Creating a Sale

  1. In the POS terminal, search for items using the search bar
  2. Click on an item to add it to the cart
  3. Adjust quantity using the + / − buttons
  4. The totals update automatically (Subtotal, Discount, Tax, Total)
  5. Apply a transaction-level discount if needed
  6. Click Pay when ready

8.3 Processing Payment

  1. Select Payment Method: Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer, Credit, or Multiple
  2. Enter the Amount Tendered
  3. The system calculates Change automatically
  4. For GCash/Bank Transfer: enter the Reference Number
  5. For split payments: add multiple payment lines
  6. Click Complete Sale
Stock is automatically deducted when payment is processed (sale status changes to PAID).

Sale Status Workflow

OPEN → PAID → CLOSED
StatusMeaningActions Available
OPENCart in progressAdd/remove items, Pay
PAIDPayment received, stock deductedPrint receipt, Close, Void
CLOSEDFinalizedPrint receipt, Void
VOIDEDCancelledView only

8.4 Voiding a Sale

  1. Go to Sales → Sales History
  2. Find the sale and click Void
  3. Enter a Void Reason (required)
  4. Confirm the void
Voiding a PAID or CLOSED sale reverses the stock deduction. A void reason is required and logged in the audit trail.

8.5 Receipts

Receipts can be viewed and printed for any PAID or CLOSED sale:

  • After payment, a receipt preview is shown
  • Click Print to print via browser dialog
  • Receipts can be reprinted anytime from Sales History

Receipt Contents (BIR-compliant)

  • Business name, TIN, and address
  • Receipt/OR number (sequential)
  • Date, time, and cashier name
  • Items with quantity, price, and amount
  • Subtotal, discount, VAT breakdown, total
  • Payment method, amount tendered, change

Senior Citizen / PWD Discount

Select SC/PWD discount type to apply:

  • 20% discount on applicable items
  • Automatically VAT-exempt (per Philippine law RA 9994 / RA 7277)

12. Audit Trail

Every change in the system is automatically recorded.

The audit trail shows:

  • Timestamp — When the change happened
  • User — Who made the change
  • Entity — What was changed (Item, Category, Sale, etc.)
  • Action — CREATE, UPDATE, or DELETE
  • Details — Field-level changes (old value → new value)

Access from Administration → Audit Trail. Filter by entity, user, date range, or action.

14. FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q: Why can't I see certain menu items?

Your user account may not have the required permissions. Contact your administrator to grant access.

Q: Why is my stock not updating after a stock-in?

Stock transactions require approval. Go to Transactions, find the CREATED transaction, and click Approve.

Q: Can I edit a completed sale?

No. Completed (PAID/CLOSED) sales cannot be edited. You must Void the sale and create a new one. This maintains audit integrity.

Q: Do I need a printer to use the POS?

No. The system works fully without a printer. Receipts are displayed on-screen and can be printed via the browser's print dialog when needed.

Q: What happens when I void a sale?

If the sale was PAID or CLOSED, the stock quantities are automatically restored. A void reason is required and recorded in the audit trail.

Q: How do I apply Senior Citizen / PWD discount?

In the POS terminal, select SC/PWD as the discount type. This automatically applies 20% discount and makes the sale VAT-exempt per Philippine law.

Q: What is a Z-Reading vs X-Reading?

  • Z-Reading — End-of-day sales summary (Daily Sales Report)
  • X-Reading — Per-shift sales summary (Shift Report)

Q: How do I reset a forgotten password?

An administrator can reset your password from Administration → Users → Reset Password.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The system is responsive and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. The POS terminal is optimized for touch screens.
