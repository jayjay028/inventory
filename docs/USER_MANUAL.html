1. Getting Started

1.1 Logging In

  1. Open your browser and navigate to the system URL
  2. Enter your Username and Password
  3. Click Sign In
Your administrator will provide your login credentials. Change your password after first login.

Default Accounts

UsernamePasswordRole
adminadminAdministrator (full access)

1.2 Navigation & Layout

The system uses a three-panel layout:

PanelPositionPurpose
Left SidebarLeft (fixed, 250px)Main navigation menu with collapsible groups + recent transactions
Main ContentCenterActive page (forms, tables, dashboard)
Quick Access PanelRight (slide-in)Quick links to Transactions, Reports, Settings, Administration

Left Sidebar Menu

  • Dashboard — Business overview and metrics
  • POS Terminal — Full-screen cashier interface
  • Inventory — Items, Categories, Stock In/Out/Adjust, Transactions
  • Sales — Sales History, Shifts
  • Contacts — Customers, Suppliers
  • Reports — PDF report generation
  • Administration — Users, Add-ons, Settings, Audit Trail

At the bottom of the left sidebar, you'll see Recent Transactions — the last 8 stock transactions with their status and relative time (e.g., "5m ago").

Click the hamburger icon (☰) in the top-left to collapse the left sidebar on smaller screens.

1.3 Quick Access Panel (Right Sidebar)

The Quick Access Panel is a slide-in panel on the right side of the screen. Click the tab on the right edge to open it.

It contains expandable sections:

SectionContains
TransactionsAll Transactions, Stock In, Stock Out, Stock Adjustment, Stock Overview, Sales History, Shifts
ReportsGrouped by: Inventory (Stock Level, Movement, Low Stock, Valuation), Financial (Sales Summary, Gross Profit, VAT Summary), POS (Z-Reading, X-Reading, Cashier Sales)
InventoryItems, Categories, Suppliers, Customers
SettingsCompany Info, Tax Settings, Document Numbering, POS Settings, Discount Settings, Access Rights
AdministrationUsers, Add-ons, Audit Trail

How it works:

  1. Click the arrow tab on the right edge of the screen
  2. The panel slides open
  3. Click a section heading (e.g., "Transactions") to expand it
  4. Click a sub-item to navigate directly to that page
  5. Only one section is expanded at a time (accordion behavior)
The Quick Access Panel is permission-filtered — you only see sections your role has access to.

3. Categories

Categories organize your items (e.g., Electronics, Food & Beverages, Office Supplies).

Create a Category

  1. Go to Inventory → Categories (left sidebar) or Quick Access → Inventory → Categories
  2. Click New Category
  3. Enter the category Name (required) and optional Description
  4. Click Create

Edit / Deactivate

  • Click the pencil icon to edit a category
  • Click the × icon to deactivate (hides from dropdowns, items remain assigned)
Deactivated categories are not deleted — they can be reactivated anytime.

4. Items

Items are the products you buy, sell, and track in inventory.

Create an Item

  1. Go to Inventory → Items
  2. Click New Item
  3. Fill in the fields:

NameYesItem name (e.g., USB Cable Type-C)
CategoryYesSelect from dropdown
UnitYesUnit of measure (pcs, kg, box, etc.)
Selling PriceYesPrice you sell at (₱)

Filtering & Search

Use the search bar to find items by name or code. Use the category dropdown filter to show items in a specific category.

5. Customers

Manage customers for sales transactions, Official Receipts (OR), and Sales Invoices (SI).

Create a Customer

  1. Go to Contacts → Customers or Quick Access → Inventory → Customers
  2. Click New Customer
  3. Fill in: Name (required), TIN, Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email
  4. Click Create
Customers are needed when issuing Official Receipts (OR) or Sales Invoices (SI) per BIR requirements.

6. Suppliers

Manage suppliers for purchase tracking and stock-in transactions.

Create a Supplier

  1. Go to Contacts → Suppliers or Quick Access → Inventory → Suppliers
  2. Click New Supplier
  3. Fill in: Name (required), TIN, Address, Contact Person, Contact Number, Email
  4. Click Create

7. Stock Operations

Stock Overview

Go to Inventory → Stock Overview or Quick Access → Transactions → Stock Overview to see current stock levels.

Stock status indicators:

  • NORMAL — Quantity above reorder level
  • LOW — At or below reorder level
  • OUT OF STOCK — Zero quantity

7.1 Stock In (Receiving Goods)

Create Transaction → Status: CREATED → Approve → Stock Updated ✓
  1. Go to Inventory → Stock In or Quick Access → Transactions → Stock In
  2. Select the Item (current stock shown)
  3. Enter Quantity and Unit Cost
  4. Optionally select a Supplier
  5. Choose Document Type (PO, RR, or None)
  6. Toggle Apply Tax if applicable
  7. Add Remarks and Add-ons (shipping, handling) if needed
  8. Click Submit
Stock is NOT updated immediately. The transaction must be Approved first.

7.2 Stock Out (Releasing Goods)

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Out or Quick Access → Transactions → Stock Out
  2. Select the Item (validates sufficient stock)
  3. Enter Quantity and Unit Price
  4. Optionally select a Customer
  5. Apply Discount if applicable (Fixed or Percentage)
  6. Choose Document Type (OR, SI, DR, or None)
  7. Click Submit

7.3 Stock Adjustment

Use for physical count corrections or inventory adjustments.

  1. Go to Inventory → Stock Adjustment
  2. Select the Item
  3. Enter the correct Quantity
  4. Add a Reason/Remarks (required for audit)
  5. Click Submit

7.4 Transaction Approval

All stock transactions go through an approval workflow:

StatusMeaningStock Impact
CREATEDPending approvalNone
APPROVEDApproved and processedStock updated
CANCELLEDCancelled/rejectedNone

To approve or cancel:

  1. Go to Inventory → Transactions or Quick Access → Transactions → All Transactions
  2. Filter by Status: CREATED
  3. Click Approve or Cancel on the transaction
Requires APPROVE_TRANSACTIONS or CANCEL_TRANSACTIONS permission.

8. POS Terminal

The Point of Sale terminal is a full-screen cashier interface for processing sales. It has a professional layout with product search on the left and the cart on the right.

8.1 Shift Management

Before making sales, you must open a shift:

Opening a Shift

  1. Navigate to POS Terminal (left sidebar or Quick Access → Transactions → Sales History)
  2. If no shift is open, you'll be prompted to open one
  3. Enter the Opening Amount (cash in drawer)
  4. Click Open Shift

Closing a Shift

  1. Go to Sales → Shifts
  2. Count your cash and enter the Closing Amount
  3. The system calculates Expected Amount and shows any Over/Short
  4. Add optional Remarks
  5. Click Close Shift
Only ONE shift can be open per cashier at a time.

8.2 Creating a Sale

  1. In the POS terminal, use the search bar (with barcode icon) to find items
  2. Click on a product card to add it to the cart
  3. Adjust quantity using the + / − buttons in the cart
  4. The totals update automatically (Subtotal, Discount, Tax, Total)
  5. Apply a transaction-level discount if needed (No Discount / % / Fixed)
  6. Click Pay when ready

8.3 Processing Payment

  1. Select Payment Method: Cash, GCash, Bank Transfer, Credit, or Multiple
  2. Enter the Amount Tendered
  3. The system calculates Change automatically
  4. For GCash/Bank Transfer: enter the Reference Number
  5. For split payments: add multiple payment lines
  6. Click Complete Sale
Stock is automatically deducted when payment is processed (sale status changes to PAID).

Sale Status Workflow

OPEN → PAID → CLOSED
StatusMeaningActions Available
OPENCart in progressAdd/remove items, Pay
PAIDPayment received, stock deductedPrint receipt, Close, Void
CLOSEDFinalizedPrint receipt, Void
VOIDEDCancelledView only

8.4 Voiding a Sale

  1. Go to Sales → Sales History
  2. Find the sale and click Void
  3. Enter a Void Reason (required)
  4. Confirm the void
Voiding a PAID or CLOSED sale reverses the stock deduction. A void reason is required and logged in the audit trail.

8.5 Receipts

Receipts can be viewed and printed for any PAID or CLOSED sale.

Receipt Contents (BIR-compliant)

  • Business name, TIN, and address
  • Receipt/OR number (sequential)
  • Date, time, and cashier name
  • Items with quantity, price, and amount
  • Subtotal, discount, VAT breakdown, total
  • Payment method, amount tendered, change

Senior Citizen / PWD Discount

Select SC/PWD discount type to apply:

  • 20% discount on applicable items
  • Automatically VAT-exempt (per Philippine law RA 9994 / RA 7277)

This tab allows you to configure default permission presets for each role. It shows a matrix table:

ColumnsDescription
Rows25 individual permissions grouped by category (Dashboard, Inventory, POS, Contacts, Administration)

How to use:

Changes here set the default permissions for newly created users. To change an existing user's permissions, edit them in User Management.

14. FAQ & Troubleshooting

Q: Where is the Quick Access panel?

Look for the small arrow tab on the right edge of your screen. Click it to slide open the Quick Access panel with shortcuts to Transactions, Reports, Settings, and more.

Q: How do I configure user access rights?

Go to Settings → User Access Rights tab (the 6th tab). Or use the Quick Access panel → Settings → Access Rights. This shows a matrix of all permissions × roles.

Q: Why can't I see certain menu items?

Your user account may not have the required permissions. Both the left sidebar and the Quick Access panel are filtered by your permissions. Contact your administrator to grant access.

Q: Why is my stock not updating after a stock-in?

Stock transactions require approval. Go to Transactions (via left sidebar or Quick Access → Transactions → All Transactions), find the CREATED transaction, and click Approve.

Q: Can I edit a completed sale?

No. Completed (PAID/CLOSED) sales cannot be edited. You must Void the sale and create a new one. This maintains audit integrity.

Q: Do I need a printer to use the POS?

No. The system works fully without a printer. Receipts are displayed on-screen and can be printed via the browser's print dialog when needed.

Q: What happens when I void a sale?

If the sale was PAID or CLOSED, the stock quantities are automatically restored. A void reason is required and recorded in the audit trail.

Q: How do I apply Senior Citizen / PWD discount?

In the POS terminal, select SC/PWD as the discount type. This automatically applies 20% discount and makes the sale VAT-exempt per Philippine law.

Q: What is a Z-Reading vs X-Reading?

  • Z-Reading — End-of-day sales summary (Daily Sales Report)
  • X-Reading — Per-shift sales summary (Shift Report)

Q: How do I reset a forgotten password?

An administrator can reset your password from Administration → Users → Reset Password.

Q: Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The system is responsive and works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. The left sidebar collapses on mobile, and the POS terminal adapts to smaller screens.

Q: How do I navigate faster between sections?

Use the Quick Access panel (right sidebar). It provides direct links to all subsections without navigating through nested menus.
